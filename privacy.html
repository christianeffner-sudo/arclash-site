ARCLASH

Privacy Policy · last updated 11 November 2026

Short version: Arclash does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who is responsible

Arclash is made by Christian Effner, Breitlacherstraße 86, 60489 Frankfurt am Main, Germany. Contact: christian.effner@googlemail.com.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, Daily Run results, settings (sound, music, haptics) and whether you own Arclash Plus are saved only on your iPhone. They never leave the device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Arclash Plus is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Sharing your score

When you tap Share, Arclash creates a score picture and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes; Arclash itself sends nothing anywhere.

No third parties

Arclash contains no third-party libraries, analytics or advertising tools. It does not use the internet except for the App Store purchase handled by iOS.

Children

Because Arclash collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Since we process no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. If you have questions, write to christian.effner@googlemail.com.

Changes

If Arclash ever starts processing data, this page will be updated before that version is released.
