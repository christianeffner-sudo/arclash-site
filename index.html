ARCLASH

HOLD · SWING · FLING

A one-finger neon arcade climber for iPhone. Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to christian.effner@googlemail.com – usually answered within a few days.

How to play

Hold anywhere on the screen: your tether fires to the anchor marked with brackets. Keep holding to swing, let go to fling yourself in the direction you are moving – best on the upswing. Hold again in the air to grab the next anchor. The tether damages enemies it touches; touching an enemy or a shot, or falling off the bottom of the screen, ends the run.

Questions

I don't hear any sound.

Arclash follows your iPhone's silent mode. Switch silent mode off (Ring/Silent switch or Action button) and check the volume. Sound and music can also be turned off in the game's settings.

How do I restore Arclash Plus on a new iPhone?

Open the Plus page (sparkle button in the menu) and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with. With Family Sharing, family members get it too.

When does a new Daily Run start?

Every day at midnight UTC (1:00 in Germany in winter, 2:00 in summer). Everyone plays the same level that day.

Does Arclash collect data or show ads?

No. No ads, no tracking, no accounts. See the privacy policy.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
